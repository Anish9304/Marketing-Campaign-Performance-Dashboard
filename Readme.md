# Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze the performance of marketing campaigns and support data-driven business decisions.

The dashboard provides insights into key marketing metrics such as **Revenue, Marketing Spend, ROI, Campaign Performance, Regional Performance, and Campaign Type Distribution**.

It helps identify high-performing campaigns and compare marketing investments with business outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer important business questions such as:

* Which marketing campaign performs the best?
* How much revenue is generated from marketing activities?
* How much is being spent on campaigns?
* Which regions generate higher revenue?
* Which campaigns provide better ROI?
* How does campaign performance vary across campaign types?
* Where should the business focus its future marketing budget?

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue and Spend by Region

This visual compares **Total Marketing Spend and Total Revenue across different regions**, including:

* Africa
* Asia
* North America
* Europe
* Oceania
* South America

This helps identify regions where marketing investment is generating stronger business results.

### 2. Campaign Spend and ROI Analysis

The dashboard compares:

* Total Spend
* Average ROI
* Campaign Name

This allows users to evaluate the effectiveness of different marketing campaigns.

### 3. ROI by Campaign

A ranking of campaigns based on their ROI helps identify the strongest and weakest performing campaigns.

Key campaigns include:

* Influencer Marketing
* Social Media Ads
* Search Engine Ads
* Content Marketing
* Radio Ads
* Billboards
* Email Marketing
* TV Commercials

### 4. Campaign Type Distribution

Campaigns are categorized into:

* Digital
* Traditional

The dashboard provides a percentage distribution of campaign types.

### 5. Revenue by Campaign

This visual compares the revenue generated by different marketing campaigns and helps identify campaigns contributing the most to business revenue.

## 📐 Key Metrics Used

### Total Revenue

```DAX
Total Revenue = SUM(Data[Revenue])
```

### Total Spend

```DAX
Total Spend = SUM(Data[Spend])
```

### Average ROI

```DAX
Average ROI = AVERAGE(Data[ROI])
```

### ROI

```text
ROI = (Revenue - Cost) / Cost
```
